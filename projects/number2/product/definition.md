# Product Definition — National Tele-Mental Health Initiative Platform

## Product name and one-line description
**National Tele-Mental Health Initiative (National Tele-MANAS) — Software Development and Platform Support Services** — an IT services engagement delivered under RFP IIITB/EHRC/2022/IT-01 to build and support software and platform infrastructure for India's national tele-mental-health program, anchored by IIITB's E-Health Research Centre in collaboration with NIMHANS and the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MOHFW). [DOC-B585FB4B498A]

## Problem statement
India's underserved and marginalised populations have limited affordability, accessibility, and availability of mental-health services. The MOHFW needs a continuously operating national tele-mental-health capability (Tele-MANAS) that is not curtailed except by force majeure, sub-optimal outcomes, merger with another national initiative, or other unforeseen circumstances [DOC-B585FB4B498A]. IIITB/EHRC therefore requires a software development and platform-support partner that can deliver, maintain, and evolve the digital systems underpinning this initiative while protecting highly sensitive health, patient, and operational data [DOC-B585FB4B498A].

- **IIITB / E-Health Research Centre** — sponsoring department that owns the contract and acts as the primary interface with the bidder [DOC-B585FB4B498A].

- **MOHFW** — the apex government stakeholder under whose aegis the initiative runs [DOC-B585FB4B498A].
- **End users (indirect)** — under-served and marginalised population groups in India (and "similar regions across the world") who are the eventual beneficiaries of the telehealth delivery platforms and models [DOC-B585FB4B498A].
