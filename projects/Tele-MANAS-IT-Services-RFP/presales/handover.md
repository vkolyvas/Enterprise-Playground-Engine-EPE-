## 1. Executive Summary

The E-Health Research Centre at IIIT Bangalore has issued an open, offline RFP (IIITB/EHRC/2022/IT-01, 25-Oct-2022) for Software Development and Platform Support Services for the National Tele-Mental Health Initiative (Tele-MANAS). The engagement is an initial 3-year IT services contract with yearly renewals, anchored in Bangalore, requiring a security-aligned (ISO 27001 or equivalent) delivery model spanning UI, functional and back-end development, integration testing, migration/enhancements/upgrades, platform support with shift-based Level 1/Level 2 coverage, and confidentiality controls over IIITB/Government/NIMHANS information including health/patient records.
