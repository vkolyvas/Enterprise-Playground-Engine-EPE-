# Product Definition: Tele-MANAS IT Services RFP

## Product Name and One-Line Description
**Product:** National Tele-Mental Health Initiative — Software Development and Platform Support Services (RFP IIITB/EHRC/2022/IT-01, 25-Oct-2022). [DOC-B585FB4B498A]
**One-line description:** An IT services offering for the design, development, integration, testing, maintenance and platform support of the National Tele-MANAS tele-mental health software and infrastructure, delivered to IIITB's E-Health Research Centre under MOHFW. [DOC-B585FB4B498A]

## Problem Statement
India needs to extend mental-healthcare reach to under-served and marginalised populations using Information and Communication Technologies (ICTs), improving affordability, accessibility and availability of technology solutions covering devices, health data, and delivery platforms/models. Current IIITB/EHRC focus areas are mental health, malnutrition and disabilities, and a national-scale Tele-MANAS platform requires a qualified bidder to build and continuously support the underlying software/hardware stack. [DOC-B585FB4B498A]
